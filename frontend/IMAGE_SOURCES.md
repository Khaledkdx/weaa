# Interior imagery

The `public/images/interior-*.webp` files are original AI-generated illustrative
photographs created for this WEAA preview. They do not depict actual WEAA
facilities, vehicles, employees, customers, or companies listed for sale.

Each default image is assigned to one public page or content position. Company
marketplace imagery is labeled "صورة توضيحية" in the interface. Editors can
replace the defaults with distinct, authorized HTTPS image URLs in the CMS.
